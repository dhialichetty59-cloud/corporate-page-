# Career Excel Academy corporate training prototype

This is a dependency-free, static prototype based on the supplied corporate training brief. Open `index.html` in a browser to review it. The package builder opens with an introduction, a three-step overview and a "Start your build" button. Visitors can then select modules across three training sections, specify a cohort of at least five people, choose a delivery method, optionally add preferred timing and review an on-screen package summary. The review step collects the corporate contact's name, organisation and work email, then the Submit package button displays a thank-you notice showing where a package-response email would be sent. This is only a prototype: no request or email is sent or saved. A live submission and email service must be connected before publication; the proposed 24-hour response time must also be confirmed by CEA.

## Publishing notes

- The corporate hero now links directly to a featured Category B / Internship / Mixed-Mode section, highlighting the four Higher Certificate areas and the 30 April 2027 WSP deadline supplied for the campaign. CEA must confirm qualification names, NQF levels, Category B eligibility, delivery and any B-BBEE or Mining Charter applicability before publishing compliance claims.
- The package builder collects the contact name, organisation and work email required for a live follow-up, but it does not yet transmit or store those details. A live submission/email service must be connected before publication.
- The logo is loaded from CEA's public website, the typefaces load from Google Fonts, and the hero photograph loads from Unsplash. These visual assets require an internet connection.
- Headings use Outfit and other text uses Lato, matching the fonts observed on CEA's live website. Both fonts are loaded by the standalone page for a consistent preview.
- The page palette follows CEA's live-site navy and gold/orange brand colours, with white and neutral grey/pale backgrounds for readable text and surfaces.
- The NQF 5/NQF 6 Diploma split is a provisional interpretation of the supplied brief: Year 1 is grouped under NQF 5 and Years 2–3 under NQF 6. CEA must confirm these mappings and the exact module catalogue.
- Diploma options are presented as expandable cards; selecting a Diploma reveals its NQF levels and individual module checkboxes grouped by study year, and selecting it again collapses the options. The brief says Construction Project Management Years 2 and 3 are customisable but does not list those modules; the builder flags those years for confirmation with CEA.
- Professional short courses also use expandable programme cards; each opens its brief-based topic groups and selectable options.
- The QCTO programmes path first offers Occupational Skills Programmes or Part and Full Qualifications, then displays selectable options from the corresponding brief category.
- Practical skills uses the same expandable-card hierarchy across the section: each main heading opens a panel of subcategory cards and selectable options. Machinery & Operator Licensing contains both lifting/material-handling and plant machinery cards; HSE and Core Trade contain their respective subcategory cards, including the Safety Management / Junior Safety Officer Track under Core Trade. Options reflect the detailed lists on pages 11–12 of the supplied brief.
- The supplied brief and requested section label differ on the accreditation/credit status of QCTO programmes. The prototype preserves the requested non-credited label, with a reminder that CEA must confirm the approved classification before publication.
- The page can be hosted as a standalone static page if the hosting account allows uploading HTML, CSS and JavaScript files. That would not automatically add the page to the WordPress navigation or reuse the WordPress theme/header/footer.
- To make this a native section of the current WordPress site, the site administrator or developer will still need to add the page to WordPress, connect it to the site's navigation and confirm the final design in the live theme.
- Before publication, CEA should verify the course wording, module names, accreditation details, delivery options and contact route against its current approved information.
